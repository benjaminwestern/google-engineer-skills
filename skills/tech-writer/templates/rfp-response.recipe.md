# RFP Response Recipe

**Medium:** Google Docs (`gws-docs`)

## Purpose
An RFP (Request for Proposal) Response is a highly persuasive, competitive document. Its purpose is to prove that your solution is the only logical choice for the evaluator's specific situation. It must be outcome-driven, clear, and confident.

## Process
1.  **Define Win Themes:** Before drafting, work with the user to establish 3 to 5 outcome-driven Win Themes. These are the core reasons the evaluator should choose your solution based on their priorities.
2.  **Establish Ghosting Strategy:** Identify areas where competitors are known to be weak and ensure those topics are subtly highlighted as strengths in your response.
3.  **Generate Outline:** Propose a structure mapped to the RFP requirements.
4.  **Draft Section by Section:** Use `todowrite` to track progress and `gws-docs` to build the document.

## Structure

### 1. Executive Summary
Lead with the primary recommendation immediately. Follow with your Win Themes as the supporting arguments (The Pyramid Principle). Do not use corporate fluff or jargon. Address the business value directly.

### 2. Business Understanding
Demonstrate a deep understanding of the evaluator's specific situation. Do not just describe your capabilities; show how your capabilities have been built specifically to solve their problems.

### 3. Proposed Solution
Detail the technical solution. Ensure that every technical feature described is explicitly linked to a business outcome or ROI. Do not overwhelm the evaluator with implementation minutiae unless explicitly requested.

### 4. Requirement Responses
Answer specific RFP requirements directly, confidently, and concisely.
*   State Comply, Partially Comply, or Do Not Comply clearly (if required by the format).
*   Follow immediately with the justification.
*   Thread your Win Themes throughout these answers. If a requirement maps to a competitor's weakness, lean heavily into your ghosting strategy here.

### 5. Implementation Approach & Timeline
Provide a realistic, structured plan for delivering the solution. Focus on time-to-value.

## Style Constraints
- Use British English.
- Use an active, confident, and highly persuasive voice.
- Bullet points must be 1 to 3 complete sentences long. Do not use colons at the start of bullet points.
- Build compliance matrices and pricing tables natively in Google Docs.
- Avoid bold and italics except to highlight critical differentiators.
