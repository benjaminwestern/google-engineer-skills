# Tutorial skeleton

Voice: instructional, warm, direct. Address the reader as "you". Register: straight. A tutorial takes a reader from nothing to one concrete, working result by following steps in order. Its promise is that if they follow along, it works.

Candidate shape:

- **Title and outcome** — state exactly what the reader will have built or achieved by the end.
- **Prerequisites** — the tools, accounts, and knowledge assumed, with versions where they matter.
- **Steps** — numbered, in strict order, each a full instruction with the command or code and a sentence on what it does and why. Show expected output so the reader can confirm they are on track.
- **Checkpoints** — brief moments to verify progress, so a reader who diverged can catch it early.
- **Result** — what the reader now has, and how to confirm it works end to end.
- **Where next** — a short pointer to the related guide or reference for going deeper.

Keep each step small and verifiable. Explain the why alongside the what, so the reader learns rather than just copies. Cite external tools and services inline.
