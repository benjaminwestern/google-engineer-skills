# Blog post skeleton

Voice: first person singular, personable, opinionated. Address the reader as "you". Register: this is the one type where full playful framing fits, but still ask the user first and default to straight if they do not answer. A blog earns attention with a hook and keeps it with pace and a clear point of view.

Frontmatter extras for this type: `author` (the user's name when known), `genre` or `tags`.

Candidate shape:

- **Title** — concrete and inviting. A professional title by default. A witty or thematic one only when the user has asked for playful framing.
- **Hook** — open with a scene, a question the reader feels, or a punchy claim. Earn the next paragraph.
- **The point** — say what you actually believe, early. Blogs reward a clear stance over a balanced survey.
- **The body** — carry the argument in short, one-thought-per-line paragraphs to keep pace. When the register is playful, this is where an extended analogy or a narrative frame can do real explanatory work, as long as the substance underneath stays rigorous. Break long blocks at their natural seams.
- **The turn** — acknowledge the counter-case or the nuance, then land your position anyway.
- **Close** — a short reflection or a call to engage. A blog can end by inviting a reply.

Emoji heading markers suit this type when the register is lighter. Keep them tasteful, not one on every heading. Use ellipses and rhetorical-question headings sparingly. Cite only where a reader would want to verify a specific fact.
